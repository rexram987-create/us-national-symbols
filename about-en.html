About the Project and the Researcher

How a passion for history, biographies, and advanced research tools merged to create the ultimate archive of American National Symbols.

The Mission: Re-accessing History

This project was born from the understanding that symbols are the visual language of history. They capture within them struggles, triumphs, and the values that shape a nation. However, the information regarding them is often scattered, incomplete, or lacking the etymological depth that allows an understanding of their true origins.

Our goal isn't just to present a list of symbols. The mission is to dig deep into the National Archives (NARA), the Library of Congress, and the Smithsonian Institution, and provide the Israeli and international audience with profound, accurate research, without taking any "shortcuts". Every document on this site was designed and planned to provide comprehensive answers – from the historical identity of the designers and official adoption to the exact meaning of every word in the extended etymology.

We believe that understanding national symbols is essential to understanding the political and social culture of the United States, especially in an era of global changes, where national identity is being re-examined.

About the Researcher (Ram)

A researcher and bibliophile who dedicates his free time to deeply reading history books, science, biographies, and historical novels. This passion for exploring the past, combined with technological understanding, led to the development of this project.

The research and development of the site were carried out using advanced artificial intelligence tools, acting as a historical "research assistant" to verify sources, analyze complex etymologies, and design a clean, modern, and accessible user environment.
