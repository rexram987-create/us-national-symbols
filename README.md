## English

A bilingual, visually rich static website dedicated to the national symbols, civic icons, historical documents, natural emblems, and cultural symbols of the United States of America.

The project presents an educational digital archive about the symbols that shaped American identity, civic values, national mythology, and historical memory — from the Declaration of Independence, the Constitution, and the American flag, to the Statue of Liberty, Mount Rushmore, the bald eagle, the American bison, and additional cultural symbols.

### 📖 Project Overview

**National Symbols Archive** is an educational and historical web project that presents the symbols shaping the identity, values, mythology, and civic memory of the United States.

The archive is organized into three major categories:

1. **The Vision & The Law**  
   Foundational civic documents and ideals, including the Declaration of Independence, the Constitution, the Bill of Rights, and the national motto.

2. **National Identity & History**  
   Major historical and identity symbols such as the American flag, the national anthem, the Great Seal, Uncle Sam, the Liberty Bell, the Statue of Liberty, and Mount Rushmore.

3. **Nature, Folklore & Culture**  
   Natural and cultural symbols such as the bald eagle, American bison, oak tree, rose, apple pie, wild turkey, and mustang.

- 🎨 Premium dark visual design using navy and gold tones.
- 🖼️ Image-based symbol cards with hover effects.

- 🖋️ Google Fonts integration using Heebo and Playfair Display.
